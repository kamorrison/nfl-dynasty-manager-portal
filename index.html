How to Submit Decisions

Use the Submit Decision buttons on your team page or the Contract Decision Board. Team pages now include a multi-year contract matrix so you can evaluate trade cost, expiration timing, and likely next options before you submit. The form will pre-fill your team and player when possible. Decisions are not official until reviewed and approved by the commissioner. If you submit a correction, the latest approved submission before the deadline will be used.
